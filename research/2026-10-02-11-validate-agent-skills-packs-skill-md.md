## 3️⃣ Risks & mitigations (quick checklist)

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| **Low perceived value vs free GitHub skills** | Bundle skills into a *workflow* (e.g., “SEO‑audit → code‑review → deployment”) and highlight time‑saved metrics. |
| **Price too low to cover creation cost** | Start at $19 (covers basic authoring time); consider a “basic / premium” tier ($19 / $39) after validating demand. |
| **Buyers don’t know how to install SKILL.md** | Provide a one‑click install script, a short video tutorial, and a “FAQ” page; list install steps on the product page. |
| **Platform fee erosion (Gumroad 5 % + 30¢ per transaction)** | Price at $19 → after fees you keep ~$18.1; at $39 → ~$36.9. Model cash‑flow accordingly. |
| **Competition from free directories** | Emphasise *curated*, *tested* packs with lifetime updates and a refund policy (14‑day money‑back). |
| **Uncertain demand** | Run a pre‑sale / “coming‑soon” landing page *before* building the full pack; collect email sign‑ups and convert to early‑bird purchases. |

## 4️⃣ Recommended immediate actions (within the Kiraci system)

### Bottom line
- **Demand exists** (people search for price info, a $100 bounty is posted, a few paid packs sell), but **the market is fragmented** and **most skill sharing is free**.  
- A **$10‑$30 price point** is viable **only if** the pack is tightly themed, well‑documented, and differentiated from the many free repositories.  
- **Start small**: a minimal viable pack (3‑5 skills) sold for $19 on Gumroad, with a 14‑day refund, gives you the fastest feedback loop and lets you update the 3‑month sales forecast with real data.
