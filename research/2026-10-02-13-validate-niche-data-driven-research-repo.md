## 3. Risks (devil’s‑advocate view)

| Risk | Why it matters | Mitigation (high‑level) |
|------|----------------|------------------------|
| **Weak actual demand** – the “$1 000 / month” HN chatter is about SaaS tools, not research reports. | May over‑estimate willingness to pay $39 for a niche report. | Run a **landing‑page pre‑sale** before building any API pipelines. |
| **Low differentiation** – several AI tools already produce cheap reports. | Buyers may stick with familiar platforms. | Emphasise **NAICS‑by‑county granularity**, **real‑time Census CBP + USAspending data**, and **transparent data‑source citations**. |
| **Data‑licensing / API cost** – free Census APIs have rate limits; USAspending/SEC EDGAR may require paid access. | Cost per report could exceed the $39 price, eroding margin. | Prototype with **free** datasets first; if needed, negotiate **data‑reseller** contracts or use aggregated public summaries. |
| **Payment‑processor fees** – 5 % + $0.50 per $39 sale = $2.45, leaving $36.5 gross. | May make the venture marginal if volume is low. | Consider **subscription** model (e.g., $9/month for 3 reports) to amortise fees. |
| **Trust in AI‑generated insights** – buyers may prefer human analysts. | High refund rates or low repeat purchase. | Include **data provenance** (list every source URL used), offer a **free preview** (first page) and a **money‑back guarantee** for the first 30 days. |
