# Kiraci Product Disclosure Policy

## Policy Statement
All Kiraci products must include a clear and conspicuous disclosure about their AI-assisted nature. This disclosure satisfies:
- FTC "clear and conspicuous" standard for disclosures
- EU AI Act Article 50 "first exposure" transparency requirement
- Etsy-style marketplace disclosure practices

## Core Requirements
1. **Mandatory Placement**: The disclosure must appear:
   - Above the purchase button on product pages
   - Within delivered product files (e.g., README, documentation)
   - In all marketing copy (social media, emails, advertisements)

2. **Prohibited Claims**: Never claim human authorship or imply the product was created solely by humans.

3. **Required Elements**: Each disclosure must include:
   - AI agent name(s) involved in creation
   - Human review scope (if any occurred)
   - Sources used during creation
   - Creation date (YYYY-MM-DD format)

## Disclosure Template
Use this exact template for all Kiraci products. Replace bracketed placeholders with product-specific information.

```
How this was made
-----------------
This product was created with AI assistance. Specific details:
- AI agent(s): [Agent names, e.g., Kiraci Scout, Kiraci Builder]
- Human review: [Scope, e.g., "Accuracy verification only" or "No human review"]
- Sources: [Source description, e.g., "Public web pages, licensed databases" or "Proprietary Kiraci knowledge base"]
- Created: [YYYY-MM-DD]

Note: This is an AI-assisted product. No claim of human authorship is made.
```

## Implementation Guidelines
### Product Pages
Place the disclosure immediately above the primary call-to-action button (e.g., "Buy Now", "Download"). Use font size at least 80% of body text with sufficient color contrast (WCAG AA minimum).

### Delivered Files
Include the disclosure in:
- Top-level README.md
- Any documentation files
- As a visible watermark in generated images (where appropriate)
- In metadata of generated files

### Marketing Copy
Include a shortened version in all promotional materials:
"AI-assisted product made with [Agent Names]. See full disclosure: [Link to product page]"

## Compliance Verification
Before publishing any product, verify:
1. Disclosure appears in all three required locations
2. Template contains all four required elements
3. No human authorship claims exist anywhere
4. Font size and contrast meet accessibility standards
5. Language is unambiguous and placed where users see it before purchasing

## Policy Updates
This policy will be reviewed quarterly or when regulations change. Product owners are responsible for updating disclosures when this policy is revised.
